It is easier to buy revenue than it is to create it:

Starting a business is exciting. However, raising capital, building teams, and going to market without a proven product can be challenging.
As an alternative, I believe that the best way to grow in business is by buying existing businesses with commercial debt (growing through acquisitions)

Here is why commercial debt works:

Immediate de-risked revenue: when you build a new business, you might not have cash flow from day one, but when you buy an existing profitable business, you will be in the green from the start.

Leverage, Not Equity: Investor money is expensive and costs you equity. However, commercial debt gives you access to financing without diluting your ownership.

Operational Efficiency: established businesses come with built-in proven processes and teams, which minimizes the risk of unknown variables.

Sometimes, buying growth beats building it. Rather than reinventing the wheel, leverage one that's already rolling.
