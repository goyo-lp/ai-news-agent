From information to automation:

I strongly believe the future of AI will be more than just a chat interface. I believe AI will transition from information to automation. Teams of 5 will be able to do the work of 50, freeing up more time for creativity and connectivity.
AI will also transition to growing through vertical-specific industry applications. Whether it is in logistics, marketing, healthcare, or finance, having niche-trained models will provide more accurate and reliable results.

For example, we will start having:
- Agentic processes that automate sales development and self-improve marketing outreach.
- Agents that track and verify all the steps within a supply chain, facilitating anomaly detection and reducing fraud,
- Ways to automate Zero Knowledge Proofs, enabling ways to verify sensitive information without exposing raw data. This will be huge for healthcare.

On top of that, rapid technological advances in federated learning (training models without sharing data) and homomorphic encryption (computing data without decrypting it) will facilitate compliance and governance. This will reduce AI-privacy concerns and accelerate adoption.
