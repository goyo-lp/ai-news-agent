Graduation Challenge Completed!!

Yesterday I ran my first ever hashtag#PittsburghMarathon. By far, the hardest things I've done so far (including completing a Master's Program at Carnegie Mellon University).

Yesterday, I learned that the first half is physical, but the second is 100% mental. At mile 18, my body wanted to give up. My legs and ankles were hurting, and I had almost no breath. Nevertheless, I decided to keep going. At times, I was running with my eyes closed, trying to gather strength to keep going.

Funny enough, at mile 22, after all the pain endured, I had one of the most peaceful moments I've ever had. There was something extremely soothing about pushing my body past my perceived limit and realizing I still had much more to give.

This experience also taught me the power of support and community. During the whole 26.2 miles, there was not one single instance without people cheering for the runners (even the ones they didn't know). Experiencing the vibrancy and support of the hashtag#PittsburghCommunity made all the difference in this running experience.
