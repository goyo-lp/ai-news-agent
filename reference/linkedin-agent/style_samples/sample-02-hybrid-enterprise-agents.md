Enterprise AI Agents will need both Generative and Neuro-Symbolic intelligence.

Pushing agentic systems from prototypes into real production workflows will require a strategic implementation of logic on how to blend both Gen-AI and NeSy-AI.

Gen-AI is great at:
- Messy inputs
- Reasoning
- Adapting to new situations in real time

However, I believe NeSy-AI is better suited for:
- Predictable behavior
- Policy and compliance enforcement
- Explainable and auditable decision paths

Designing agentic systems that combine both at the enterprise level will leverage the capabilities of understanding real-world complexity while executing with precision and effectively orchestrating multi-step workflows.

It will be interesting to see if and how hybrid AI architecture is implemented in agentic, scalable systems.
